# 拉普拉斯变换的性质

为了叙述方便, 在下面的性质中, 均假设所涉及的拉氏变换存在, 且满足拉普拉斯变换的定义。

1. 线性性质：设 $\alpha, \beta$ 为常数，且有 $\mathcal{L}[f (t)] = F (s)$ ， $\mathcal{L}[g (t)] = G (s)$ ，则有
    -  $\mathcal {L} [ \alpha f(t)+ \beta g(t)] = \alpha F (s) + \beta G (s)$
    - $\mathcal {L}^{- 1} \left[ \alpha F (s) + \beta G (s) \right] = \alpha f(t)+ \beta g (t)$
2. 相似性质：设 $\mathcal{L}[f (t)] = F (s)$ ，则对任一常数 $a > 0$ 有
    - $\mathcal {L} [ f (a t) ] = \frac {1}{a} F \left (\frac {s}{a}\right)$
3. 微分性质：
    1. 导数的像函数：设 $\mathcal{L}[f (t)] = F (s)$ ，则有
        - $\mathcal {L} \left[ f^{\prime}(t)\right] = s F (s) - f (0)$
        - $\mathcal {L} \left[ f^{(n)}(t)\right] = s^{n} F (s) - s^{n - 1} f (0) - s^{n - 2} f^{\prime} (0) - \dots - f^{(n - 1)} (0)$
            - 其中， $f^{(k)}(0)$ 应理解为 $\lim_{t\to 0^{+}}f^{(k)}(t)$
    2. 像函数的导数：设 $\mathcal{L}[f (t)] = F (s)$ ，则有
        - $F^{\prime} (s) = - \mathscr {L} [ t f(t)]$
        - $F^{(n)} (s) = (- 1)^{n} \mathscr {L} [ t^{n} f(t)]$
4. 积分性质：
    1. 积分的像函数：设 $\mathcal{L}[f (t)] = F (s)$ ，则有
        - $\mathcal {L} \left[ \int_{0}^{t} f (\tau) \mathrm{d} \tau \right] = \frac {1}{s} F (s)$
        - $\mathcal {L} \left[ \underbrace {\int_{0}^{t} \mathrm{d} t \int_{0}^{t} \mathrm{d} t \cdots \int_{0}^{t}}_{n \text {次}} f(t)  \mathrm{d} t \right] = \frac {1}{s^{n}} F (s)$
    2. 像函数的积分：设 $\mathcal{L}[f (t)] = F (s)$ ，则有
        - $\int_{s}^{\infty} F (s) \mathrm{d} s = \mathcal {L} \left[ \frac {f (t)}{t} \right]$
        - $\underbrace {\int_{s}^{\infty} \mathrm{d} s \int_{s}^{\infty} \mathrm{d} s \cdots \int_{s}^{\infty}}_{n \text {次}} F (s) \mathrm{d} s = \mathcal {L} \left[ \frac {f (t)}{t^{n}} \right]$
5. 延迟性质：设 $\mathcal{L}[f (t)]=F (s)$ ，当 $t<0$ 时， $f (t)=0$ ，则对任一非负实数 $\tau$ 有
    - $\mathcal {L} [ f (t - \tau) ] = \mathrm{e}^{- s \tau} F (s)$
    - 必须注意的是本性质对 $f (t)$ 的要求，即当 $t<0$ 时 $f (t)=0$ 。此时 $f (t-\tau)$ 在 $t<\tau$ 时为零，故 $f (t-\tau)$ 应理解为 $f (t-\tau) u (t-\tau)$ ，而不是 $f (t-\tau) u (t)$ 。因此完整的写法应为
    - $\mathcal {L} [ f (t - \tau) u (t - \tau) ] = \mathrm{e}^{- s \tau} F (s)$
    - 相应地就有 $\mathcal{L}^{-1}\left[\mathrm{e}^{-s\tau}F (s)\right] = f (t - \tau) u (t - \tau).$
6. 位移性质：设 $\mathcal{L}[f (t)] = F (s)$ ，则有
    - $\mathcal {L} \left[ \mathrm{e}^{a t} f(t)\right] = F (s - a) \quad (a \text {为一复常数}).$
7. 周期函数的像函数：设 $f (t)$ 是 $[0, +\infty)$ 内以 $T$ 为周期的函数，且 $f (t)$ 在一个周期内逐段光滑，则
    - $\mathcal {L} [ f(t)] = \frac {1}{1 - \mathrm{e}^{- s T}} \int_{0}^{T} f(t)\mathrm{e}^{- s t} \mathrm{d} t$
8. 卷积定理：设 $\mathcal{L}[f (t)] = F(s)$ ， $\mathcal{L}[g(t)] = G(s)$ ，则有
    - 时域卷积定理：$\mathcal {L} \left[ f(t)* g(t)\right] = F (s) \cdot  G(s)$
    - 频域卷积定理： $\mathcal {L} \left[ f (t) \cdot g (t) \right] = \frac {1}{2 \pi j} F (s) * G (s)$

# 例题

> [!NOTE]- 例题：求 $\cos \omega t$ 的拉氏变换.
>
> 解：
>
> 由 $\cos \omega t = \frac{1}{2} (\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}\omega t} + \mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{j}\omega t})$ 及 $\mathcal{L}[\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}\omega t}] = \frac{1}{s - \mathrm{j}\omega}$ ，有$$\mathcal {L} [ \cos \omega t ] = \frac {1}{2} (\mathcal {L} [ \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j} \omega t} ] + \mathcal {L} [ \mathrm{e}^{- \mathrm{j} \omega t} ]) = \frac {1}{2} \left (\frac {1}{s - j \omega} + \frac {1}{s + j \omega}\right) = \frac {s}{s^{2} + \omega^ {2}}.$$
>
> 同样可得 $\mathcal{L}\left[\sin\omega t\right]=\frac{\omega}{s^{2}+\omega^{2}}.$

> [!NOTE]- 例题：已知 $F (s) = \frac{5s - 1}{(s + 1)(s - 2)}$ ，求 $\mathcal{L}^{-1}[F (s)]$
>
> 解：
>
> 由 $F (s) = \frac{5s - 1}{(s + 1)(s - 2)} = 2\frac{1}{s + 1} +3\frac{1}{s - 2}$ 及 $\mathcal{L}[e^{at}] = \frac{1}{s - a}$ ，有$$\mathcal {L}^{- 1} [ F (s) ] = 2 \mathcal {L}^{- 1} \left[ \frac {1}{s + 1} \right] + 3 \mathcal {L}^{- 1} \left[ \frac {1}{s - 2} \right] = 2 \mathrm{e}^{- t} + 3 \mathrm{e}^{2 t}.$$
>

> [!NOTE]- 例题：求解常微分方程$$y^{\prime \prime}(t)+ \omega^ {2} y(t)= 0, \quad y (0) = 0, \quad y^{\prime} (0) = \omega .$$
>
> 解：
>
> 对方程两边取拉氏变换, 并利用线性性质及 $\mathcal {L} \left[ f^{(n)}(t)\right] = s^{n} F (s) - s^{n - 1} f (0) - s^{n - 2} f^{\prime} (0) - \dots - f^{(n - 1)} (0)$ 有$$s^{2} Y (s) - s y (0) - y^{\prime} (0) + \omega^ {2} Y (s) = 0,$$
>
> 其中 $Y (s) = \mathcal{L}[y (t)]$ ，代入初值即得$$Y (s) = \frac {\omega}{s^{2} + \omega^ {2}}.$$
>
> 根据[[Laplace 变换#^90d59|例题]]的结果, 有 $y (t)=\mathcal{L}^{-1}[Y (s)]=\sin\omega t.$

> [!NOTE]- 例题：求 $f (t)=t^{m}$ 的拉氏变换 ( $m \geqslant 1$ 且为正整数).
>
> 解法一：直接利用定义求解.
>
> $$\begin{array}{r l} \mathcal {L} [ t^{m} ] & = \int_{0}^{+ \infty} t^{m} \mathrm{e}^{- s t} \mathrm{d} t = - \frac {1}{s} \int_{0}^{+ \infty} t^{m} \mathrm{d} (\mathrm{e}^{- s t}) \\ & = - \frac {1}{s} t^{m} \mathrm{e}^{- s t} \Bigg |_{0}^{+ \infty} + \frac {1}{s} \int_{0}^{+ \infty} \mathrm{e}^{- s t} m t^{m - 1} \mathrm{d} t, \end{array}$$
>
> 可得递推关系 $\mathcal{L}[t^m] = \frac{m}{s}\mathcal{L}[t^{m - 1}]$ ，又由 $\mathcal{L}[1] = \frac{1}{s}$ 有$$\mathcal {L} \left[ t^{m} \right] = \frac {m !}{s^{m + 1}}.$$
>
> 解法二：利用导数的像函数性质求解.
>
> 设 $f (t)=t^{m}$ ，则 $f^{(m)}(t)=m!$ 且 $f (0)=f' (0)=\cdots=f^{(m-1)}(0)=0$ ，由微分性质有 $\mathcal{L}[f^{(m)}(t)]=s^{m}\mathcal{L}[f (t)]$ ，即 $$\mathcal {L} \left[ t^{m} \right] = \frac {1}{s^{m}} \mathcal {L} \left[ m! \right] = \frac {m !}{s^{m + 1}}.$$
>

> [!NOTE]- 例题：求函数 $f (t)=t\sin\omega t$ 的拉氏变换.
>
> 解：
>
> 由于已知 $\mathcal{L}[\sin \omega t] = \frac{\omega}{s^2 + \omega^2}$ ，根据 $F^{\prime} (s) = - \mathscr {L} [ t f(t)]$ 有$$\mathcal {L} \left[ t \sin \omega t \right] = - \frac {\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d} s} \left (\frac {\omega}{s^{2} + \omega^ {2}}\right) = \frac {2 \omega s}{\left (s^{2} + \omega^ {2}\right)^{2}}.$$

> [!NOTE]- 例题：求函数 $f (t)=t^{2}\cos^{2}t$ 的拉氏变换.
>
> 解：
>
> $$\begin{array}{r l} \mathcal {L} [ t^{2} \cos^ {2} t ] & = \frac {1}{2} \mathcal {L} [ t^{2} (1 + \cos 2 t) ] = \frac {1}{2} \frac {\mathrm{d}^{2}}{\mathrm{d} s^{2}} \left (\frac {1}{s} + \frac {s}{s^{2} + 4}\right) \\ & = \frac {2 (s^{6} + 2 4 s^{2} + 3 2)}{s^{3} (s^{2} + 4)^{3}}. \end{array}$$

> [!NOTE]- 例题：求函数 $f (t)=\frac{\sin t}{t}$ 的拉氏变换.
>
> 解：
>
> 由 $\mathcal{L}[\sin t] = \frac{1}{1 + s^2}$ 及 $\int_{s}^{\infty} F (s) \mathrm{d} s = \mathcal {L} \left[ \frac {f (t)}{t} \right]$ 有$$\mathcal {L} \left[ \frac {\sin t}{t} \right] = \int_{s}^{\infty} \frac {1}{1 + s^{2}} \mathrm{d} s = \operatorname{arccot} s$$
>
> 即 $$\int_{0}^{+ \infty} \frac {\sin t}{t} \mathrm{e}^{- s t} \mathrm{d} t = \operatorname{arccot} s$$
>
> 在上式中, 如果令 $s=0$ 有 $$\int_{0}^{+ \infty} \frac {\sin t}{t} \mathrm{d} t = \frac {\pi}{2}$$

> [!NOTE]- 例题：计算积分 (1) $\int_0^{+\infty}\mathrm{e}^{-3t}\cos 2t\mathrm{d}t;$ (2) $\int_0^{+\infty}\frac{1 - \cos t}{t}\mathrm{e}^{-t}\mathrm{d}t.$
>
> 解：
>
> (1)由 $\mathcal{L}[\cos 2t] = \frac{s}{s^2 + 4}$ ，有$$\int_{0}^{+ \infty} \mathrm{e}^{- 3 t} \cos 2 t \mathrm{d} t = \frac {s}{s^{2} + 4} \Big |_{s = 3} = \frac {3}{1 3}$$
>
> (2) 由$\int_{s}^{\infty} F (s) \mathrm{d} s = \mathcal {L} \left[ \frac {f (t)}{t} \right]$有$$\begin{array}{r l} \mathcal {L} \left[ \frac {1 - \cos t}{t} \right] & = \int_{s}^{\infty} \mathcal {L} [ 1 - \cos t ] \mathrm{d} s \\ & = \int_{s}^{\infty} \frac {1}{s (s^{2} + 1)} \mathrm{d} s = \frac {1}{2} \ln \frac {s^{2}}{s^{2} + 1} \Bigg |_{s}^{\infty} = \frac {1}{2} \ln \frac {s^{2} + 1}{s^{2}}, \end{array}$$
>
> 即$$\int_{0}^{+ \infty} \frac {1 - \cos t}{t} \mathrm{e}^{- s t} \mathrm{d} t = \frac {1}{2} \ln \frac {s^{2} + 1}{s^{2}},$$
>
> 令 $s = 1$ 得$$\int_{0}^{+ \infty} \frac {1 - \cos t}{t} \mathrm{e}^{- t} \mathrm{d} t = \frac {1}{2} \ln 2.$$
>

> [!NOTE]- 例题：设 $f(t)= \sin t$ ，求 $\mathcal{L}\left[f\left (t - \frac{\pi}{2}\right)\right]$
>
> 解：
>
> 由于 $\mathcal{L}[\sin t] = \frac{1}{s^2 + 1}$ , 根据 $\mathcal {L} [ f (t - \tau) ] = \mathrm{e}^{- s \tau} F (s)$ 有 $$\mathcal {L} \left[ f \left (t - \frac {\pi}{2}\right) \right] = \mathcal {L} \left[ \sin \left (t - \frac {\pi}{2}\right) \right] = \mathrm{e}^{- \frac {\pi}{2} s} \mathcal {L} [ \sin t ] = \frac {1}{s^{2} + 1} \mathrm{e}^{- \frac {\pi}{2} s}.$$
>
> 按照前面的解释, 则应有$$\begin{array}{r l} \mathcal {L}^{- 1} \left[ \frac {1}{s^{2} + 1} \mathrm{e}^{- \frac {\pi}{2} s} \right] & = \sin \left (t - \frac {\pi}{2}\right) u \left (t - \frac {\pi}{2}\right) \\ & = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} - \cos t, & t > \frac {\pi}{2}, \\ 0, & t <   \frac {\pi}{2}. \end{array} \right. \end{array}$$

> [!NOTE]- 例题：求 $L^{-1}\left[\frac{1}{s-1}e^{-s}\right]$ .
>
> 解：
>
> 由 $\mathcal{L}^{-1}\left[\frac{1}{s - 1}\right] = \mathrm{e}^t u (t)$ ，有$$\mathcal {L}^{- 1} \left[ \frac {1}{s - 1} \mathrm{e}^{- s} \right] = \mathrm{e}^{t - 1} u (t - 1) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \mathrm{e}^{t - 1}, & t > 1, \\ 0, & t <   1. \end{array} \right.$$
>

> [!NOTE]- 例题：求全波整流后的正弦波 $f (t)=\left|\sin\omega t\right|$ 的像函数.
>
> 解：
>
> $f (t)$ 的周期为 $T = \frac{\pi}{\omega}$ , 故有$$\begin{array}{r l} \mathcal {L} [ f(t)] & = \frac {1}{1 - \mathrm{e}^{- s T}} \int_{0}^{T} \mathrm{e}^{- s t} \sin \omega t \mathrm{d} t \\ & = \frac {1}{1 - \mathrm{e}^{- s T}} \cdot \frac {\mathrm{e}^{- s t} (- s \sin \omega t - \omega \cos \omega t)}{s^{2} + \omega^ {2}} \Bigg |_{0}^{T} \\ & = \frac {\omega}{s^{2} + \omega^ {2}} \cdot \frac {1 + \mathrm{e}^{- s T}}{1 - \mathrm{e}^{- s T}} = \frac {\omega}{s^{2} + \omega^ {2}} \mathrm{cth} \frac {s \pi}{2 \omega}. \end{array}$$
>

> [!NOTE]- 例题：已知 $F (s) = \frac{s^2}{(s^2 + 1)^2}$ , 求 $f(t)= \mathcal{L}^{-1}[F (s)]$
>
> 解：
>
> 由于 $F (s) = \frac{s}{s^2 + 1} \cdot \frac{s}{s^2 + 1}, \mathcal{L}^{-1}\left[\frac{s}{s^2 + 1}\right] = \cos t$ ，故有
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{r l} f(t)& = \mathcal {L}^{- 1} [ F (s) ] = \cos t * \cos t = \int_{0}^{t} \cos \tau \cos (t - \tau) \mathrm{d} \tau \\ & = \frac {1}{2} \int_{0}^{t} [ \cos t + \cos (2 \tau - t) ] \mathrm{d} \tau = \frac {1}{2} (t \cos t + \sin t). \end{array}
> $$
>
