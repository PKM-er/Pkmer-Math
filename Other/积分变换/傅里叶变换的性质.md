## 基本性质

- 线性性质：设 $F (\omega) = \mathcal{F}[f (t)]$ ， $G (\omega) = \mathcal{F}[g (t)]$ ， $\alpha ,\beta$ 为常数，则
    - $\mathcal {F} [ \alpha f (t) + \beta g (t) ] = \alpha F (\omega) + \beta G (\omega),$
    - $\mathcal {F}^{- 1} \left[ \alpha F (\omega) + \beta G (\omega) \right] = \alpha f (t) + \beta g (t).$
- 位移性质：设 $F (\omega) = \mathcal{F}[f (t)], t_0, \omega_0$ 为实常数，则
    - $\mathcal {F} \left[ f (t - t_{0}) \right] = \mathrm{e}^{- \mathrm{j} \omega t_{0}} F (\omega)$
    - $\mathcal {F}^{- 1} \left[ F (\omega - \omega_{0}) \right] = \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j} \omega_{0} t} f (t)$
- 相似性质：设 $F (\omega) = \mathcal{F}[f (t)]$ ， $a$ 为非零常数，则
    - $\mathcal {F} [ f (a t) ] = \frac {1}{| a |} F \left (\frac {\omega}{a}\right).\tag{8.14}$
- 微分性质：
    - $\mathcal {F} [ f^{\prime} (t) ] = \mathrm{j} \omega \mathcal {F} [ f (t)]$
    - $\mathcal {F} [ f^{(n)} (t) ] = (\mathrm{j} \omega)^{n} \mathcal {F} [ f (t) ]$
- 像函数的导数公式：
    - $\frac {\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d} \omega} F (\omega) = - \mathrm{j} \mathcal {F} [ t f (t) ]$
    - $\frac {\mathrm{d}^{n} F (\omega)}{\mathrm{d} \omega^ {n}} = (- \mathrm{j})^{n} \mathcal {F} [ t^{n} f (t) ]$
- 积分性质：设 $g (t) = \int_{-\infty}^{t}f (\tau)\mathrm{d}\tau$ ，若 $\lim_{t\to +\infty}g (t) = 0$ ，则
    - $\mathcal {F} [ g (t) ] = \frac {1}{\mathrm{j} \omega} \mathcal {F} [ f (t) ]$
- 帕塞瓦尔 (Parseval) 等式：设 $F (\omega) = \mathcal{F}[f (t)]$ ，则有
    - $\int_{- \infty}^{+ \infty} f^{2} (t) \mathrm{d} t = \frac {1}{2 \pi} \int_{- \infty}^{+ \infty} | F (\omega) |^{2} \mathrm{d} \omega$

## 卷积

设实值函数 $f_{1}(t)$ 与 $f_{2}(t)$ 在 $(- \infty, +\infty)$ 内有定义. 若反常积分 $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f_{1}(\tau) f_{2}(t - \tau) \mathrm{d}\tau$ 对任何实数 $t$ 收敛, 则它定义了一个自变量为 $t$ 的函数, 称此函数为 $f_{1}(t)$ 与 $f_{2}(t)$ 的卷积, 记为 $f_{1}(t) * f_{2}(t)$ , 即

$$
f_{1} (t) * f_{2} (t) = \int_{- \infty}^{+ \infty} f_{1} (\tau) f_{2} (t - \tau) \mathrm{d} \tau .
$$

根据定义, 很容易知道卷积满足：

- 交换律：$f_{1} (t) * f_{2} (t) = f_{2} (t) * f_{1} (t)$
- 结合律：$f_{1} (t) * [ f_{2} (t) * f_{3} (t) ] = [ f_{1} (t) * f_{2} (t) ] * f_{3} (t)$
- 分配律：$f_{1} (t) * [ f_{2} (t) + f_{3} (t) ] = f_{1} (t) * f_{2} (t) + f_{1} (t) * f_{3} (t)$

> 解：
>
> 由定义有$$f (t) * g (t) = \int_{- \infty}^{+ \infty} f (\tau) g (t - \tau) \mathrm{d} \tau = \int_{- \infty}^{+ \infty} g (\tau) f (t - \tau) \mathrm{d} \tau ,$$
>
> 由图可得: 当 $t<-1$ 时, $$f (t) * g (t) = 0;$$
>
> 当 $-1 \leqslant t \leqslant 1$ 时，$$f (t) * g (t) = \int_{- 1}^{t} 1 \cdot (t - \tau)^{2} \mathrm{d} \tau = \frac {1}{3} (t + 1)^{3};$$
>
> 当 $t > 1$ 时，$$f (t) * g (t) = \int_{- 1}^{1} 1 \cdot (t - \tau)^{2} \mathrm{d} \tau = \frac {1}{3} (6 t^{2} + 2).$$
>
> 综合得$$f (t) * g (t) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 0, & t <   - 1, \\ (t + 1)^{3} / 3, & - 1 \leqslant t \leqslant 1, \\ (6 t^{2} + 2) / 3, & t > 1. \end{array} \right.$$

通过上述例子可知，卷积由反褶、平移、相乘、积分几个部分组成。即将 $g (\tau)$ 反褶平移得 $g (t - \tau) = g (-( \tau - t))$ ，再与 $f (\tau)$ 相乘求积分，因此卷积又称为褶积或卷乘。若采用图解方式，则很容易确定积分限。

## 卷积定理

设 $F(\omega) = \mathcal{F}[f(t)]$ ， $G(\omega) = \mathcal{F}[g(t)]$ ，则有

- **时域卷积定理**：$\mathcal {F} \left[ f (t) * g (t) \right] = F (\omega) \cdot G (\omega)$
- **频域卷积定理（对偶性）**：$\mathcal {F} \left[ f (t) \cdot g (t) \right] = \frac {1}{2 \pi} F (\omega) * G (\omega)$

证：由卷积与傅氏变换定义有

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \mathcal {F} [ f (t) * g (t) ] & = \int_{- \infty}^{+ \infty} f (t) * g (t) \mathrm{e}^{- \mathrm{j} \omega t} \mathrm{d} t \\ & = \int_{- \infty}^{+ \infty} \left[ \int_{- \infty}^{+ \infty} f (\tau) g (t - \tau) \mathrm{d} \tau \right] \mathrm{e}^{- \mathrm{j} \omega t} \mathrm{d} t \\ & = \int_{- \infty}^{+ \infty} f (\tau) \left[ \int_{- \infty}^{+ \infty} g (t - \tau) \mathrm{e}^{- \mathrm{j} \omega t} \mathrm{d} t \right] \mathrm{d} \tau \\ & = \int_{- \infty}^{+ \infty} f (\tau) \mathrm{e}^{- \mathrm{j} \omega \tau} \left[ \int_{- \infty}^{+ \infty} g (t - \tau) \mathrm{e}^{- \mathrm{j} \omega (t - \tau)} \mathrm{d} t \right] \mathrm{d} \tau \\ & = F (\omega) * G (\omega). \end{array}
$$

同理可证频域卷积定理.

利用卷积定理可以简化卷积计算及某些函数的傅氏变换.

> [!NOTE]- 例题：已知 $\alpha>0,\beta>0$，求下列函数的卷积$$f (t) = \frac {\sin \alpha t}{\pi t}, \quad g (t) = \frac {\sin \beta t}{\pi t}$$
>
> 解：
>
> 设 $F (\omega) = \mathcal{F}[f (t)]$ ， $G (\omega) = \mathcal{F}[g (t)]$ ，由[[傅里叶变换#^80c0b8|例题]]知：$$F (\omega) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 1, & | \omega | \leqslant \alpha , \\ 0, & | \omega | > \alpha ; \end{array} \right. \quad G (\omega) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 1, & | \omega | \leqslant \beta , \\ 0, & | \omega | > \beta . \end{array} \right.$$
> 因此有$F (\omega) \cdot G (\omega) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 1, & | \omega | \leqslant \gamma , \\ 0, & | \omega | > \gamma \end{array} \right. (\text {其中} \gamma = \min (\alpha , \beta)),$
>
> 由卷积定理有$f (t) * g (t) = \mathcal {F}^{- 1} [ F (\omega) \cdot G (\omega) ] = \frac {\sin \gamma t}{\pi t}.$

> [!NOTE]- 例题：设 $f (t)=\mathrm{e}^{-\beta t}u (t)\cos\omega_{0}t (\beta>0)$ ，求 $\mathcal{F}[f (t)]$
>
> 解：
>
> 由频域卷积定理得$$\mathcal {F} [ f (t) ] = \frac {1}{2 \pi} \mathcal {F} [ \mathrm{e}^{- \beta t} u (t) ] * \mathcal {F} [ \cos \omega_{0} t ].$$
>
> 又由[[傅里叶变换#^823901|例题a]]与[[单位冲激函数#^e9e81c|例题b]]可知 $$\begin{align}&\mathcal {F} \left[ \mathrm{e}^{- \beta t} u (t) \right] = \frac {1}{\beta + \mathrm{j} \omega}, \\ & \mathcal {F} \left[ \cos \omega_{0} t \right] = \pi \left[ \delta (\omega + \omega_{0}) + \delta (\omega - \omega_{0}) \right].\end{align}$$
>
> 因此有$$\begin{array}{r l} & {\mathcal {F} [ f (t) ]} \\ & {= \frac {1}{2 \pi} \int_{- \infty}^{+ \infty} \frac {\pi}{\beta + j \tau} [ \delta (\omega + \omega_{0} - \tau) + \delta (\omega - \omega_{0} - \tau) ] d \tau} \\ & {= \frac {1}{2} \left[ \frac {1}{\beta + j (\omega + \omega_{0})} + \frac {1}{\beta + j (\omega - \omega_{0})} \right] = \frac {\beta + j \omega}{(\beta + j \omega)^{2} + \omega_{0}^{2}}.} \end{array}$$
>
