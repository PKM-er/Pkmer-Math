K=lim⁡Δs→0∣ΔαΔs∣=∣dαds∣K=\lim_{\Delta s\rightarrow0}\Big|\frac{\Delta\alpha}{\Delta s}\Big|=\Big|\frac{d\alpha}{ds}\Big| K=Δs→0lim​​ΔsΔα​​=​dsdα​​
